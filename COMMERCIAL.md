# Commercial licence

PTG-MEM is released under the **AGPL-3.0**. That is a deliberate choice, and for
most people it costs nothing.

## You do not need a commercial licence if

* you run it on your own machine for your own work — the overwhelming majority
  of users;
* you use it internally and don't distribute it or expose it as a network
  service to third parties;
* you fork it, modify it, and publish your changes under the AGPL.

Nothing is crippled in the free version. The graph engine, the budgeted graft,
the MCP server and the intake are the same code in every case. This follows the
WinRAR principle: the free edition never compresses worse.

## You need a commercial licence if

* your company's policy forbids AGPL in the dependency tree;
* you want to embed PTG-MEM in a product you ship without publishing your own
  source;
* you want to offer it to your customers as a hosted or network-accessible
  service.

## What a commercial licence is

A file and an invoice. Perpetual, per-organisation, covering the version you
buy and every patch release of it.

**It explicitly does not include support, an SLA, a response time, or a
roadmap commitment.** That is not a negotiating position; the author does not
operate a support function, and selling a promise that won't be kept is worse
than selling nothing. If your procurement requires an SLA, the honest answer is
that this project cannot give you one, and you should either self-support or
work through an integrator.
